Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy explains how MirrorUE (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use our website, join the Pro waitlist, or contact us.

1. Who is responsible?

Data controller: Kamil Bourouiba
Contact: kbourouiba@icloud.com

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist: email address, plan choice (Pro or Fleet), optional company name and note.
  • Website: basic technical data processed by GitHub Pages (IP address, browser type, access logs) — see GitHub’s privacy policy.
  • Local storage: we may store a flag in your browser so the site remembers you already joined the waitlist (no account required).

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

3. How signups are processed

When you join the Pro waitlist, your submission is handled as follows:

  • Web3Forms (primary): the form sends your details to Web3Forms, which forwards them to the maintainer by email. Web3Forms runs server-side spam checks; we also use hCaptcha on the form. Web3Forms may retain submissions for a limited period under their policy.
  • GitHub Issues (fallback): if Web3Forms fails, a prefilled issue may open on our public GitHub repository. You confirm by clicking “Submit new issue”. The issue is auto-closed after processing. Issue content may remain visible on GitHub.

4. Why we use your data

  • To register your interest in MirrorUE Pro or Fleet
  • To contact you about early access, pricing, and product updates related to your request
  • To understand aggregate interest (plan mix, use cases)

Legal basis (EEA/UK): your consent when submitting the waitlist form.

5. Retention

Waitlist data is kept until Pro launches and follow-up is complete, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. Web3Forms may retain submissions for a limited time per their policy. GitHub issue records may persist according to GitHub’s retention rules.

6. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to operate the site and waitlist:

  • Web3Forms (form delivery and spam protection)
  • hCaptcha (captcha verification when you submit the form)
  • GitHub (site hosting; issue fallback and notifications)

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Email kbourouiba@icloud.com with the address you used to sign up.

EEA/UK residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. French users: CNIL.

8. International transfers

GitHub, Web3Forms, hCaptcha, and related providers may process data in the United States or other countries. We rely on their applicable safeguards where required by law.

9. Children

MirrorUE is a developer tool. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
