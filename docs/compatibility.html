3. Cables & Hardware Connections

  • USB-C to USB-C: Recommended for iPhone 15/16 series and modern iPads. Delivers ultra-low latency with USB 3.0 / USB 2.0 throughput.
  • Lightning to USB-C / USB-A: Supported on iPhone 14 and earlier models. Requires standard MFi-certified or official Apple cable.
  • Hubs & Docks: USB-C hubs and Thunderbolt docks are fully supported without signal degradation.

4. Known Technical Limitations

  • DRM-Protected Video: FairPlay-encrypted playback (Netflix, Apple TV+, Disney+) will display a black rectangle during video playback as required by Apple's FairPlay architecture.
  • Biometric Prompts: Face ID and Touch ID hardware prompts cannot be forged by software injection; passcodes can be entered via Mac keyboard.
