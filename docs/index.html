// faq

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mirroring and controlling your iPhone on Mac.

How do I connect and mirror my iPhone to Mac?

Connect your iPhone to your Mac via USB cable, ensure Developer Mode is turned on in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security, tap Trust This Computer on your phone, and launch MirrorUE. The 120 fps mirror starts instantly without extra software on your phone.

Does MirrorUE support 120 FPS ProMotion displays?

Yes! MirrorUE connects directly to the native CoreMediaIO pipeline with zero-copy Metal texture rendering, delivering smooth 120 frames per second on iPhone 13 Pro/14 Pro/15 Pro/16 Pro and Apple Silicon Macs.

Can I type and control the iPhone with my Mac keyboard & trackpad?

Yes. MirrorUE translates Mac mouse clicks into real capacitive touch events and maps your physical Mac keyboard (including AZERTY, QWERTY, numbers, and accents) to the iPhone with ultra-low latency.

Is MirrorUE free and open-source?

Yes, MirrorUE core is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can download the pre-built MirrorUE.dmg directly with no account or sign-up required.

What macOS and iOS versions are supported?

MirrorUE is optimized for macOS 14 (Sonoma) and macOS 15 (Sequoia) on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs, supporting paired physical iPhones running modern iOS.
