iPhone Mirroring Not Available in the EU? How to Mirror & Control Your iPhone from Mac

Published by Kamil Bourouiba · Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

The Problem: When attempting to launch native iPhone Mirroring in France, Germany, Spain, Italy, or any of the 27 EU member states, macOS displays the error message: "iPhone Mirroring is not available in your country or region."

Why Apple Blocked iPhone Mirroring in Europe

Under the European Union's Digital Markets Act (DMA), gatekeeper platforms are subject to strict interoperability and privacy regulations. In response, Apple announced a regional hold on certain macOS Sequoia and iOS 18 features—including wireless iPhone Mirroring—for users whose Apple IDs or devices are registered in the European Union.

According to Apple's official iPhone Mirroring support documentation, the feature requires compatible Apple Silicon or T2 Mac hardware running macOS Sequoia and remains currently unavailable in European Union member states.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide (Takes 2 Minutes)

  1. Download MirrorUE: Get the signed MirrorUE.dmg and drag it into your /Applications folder.
  2. Connect via USB: Plug your iPhone into your Mac using a standard Lightning or USB-C cable. Tap "Trust This Computer" on your iPhone.
  3. Enable Developer Mode: On your iPhone, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode and switch it ON (restart required by iOS).
  4. Launch MirrorUE: Open MirrorUE on your Mac. The 120 FPS mirror feed will launch instantly with full keyboard and mouse control enabled.
